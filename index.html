❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How to choose the right voice?

First choose the language based on content. Then consider gender: male for formal content, female for soft scenarios. Use voice search to filter specific tones and preview before final selection.

Can generated audio be used commercially?

Yes! All audio generated on AI Voice Studio can be used for commercial purposes without any restrictions.

What text formats are supported?

We support plain text and PDF files. Simply import your file or paste text directly in the text area.

What is the maximum text length?

Maximum 10,000 characters per generation. For longer content, you can generate multiple audio files.

How long are generated audio files saved?

Generated files are available for download immediately and stored temporarily. Download and save them to your device.

How to insert pauses in text?

Use multiple dots (...) or periods (.) to create natural pauses in speech. Punctuation affects speech rhythm.
